Contrail

A private flight tracker for two.

Contrail is a personal iPhone app that tracks upcoming flights in real time: departure and arrival times, gates, delays, the inbound aircraft and a live map. It is built for private use by its developer and a small number of invited people. It is not available on the App Store.

What it does

  • Shows live status for flights you add or import.
  • Sends alerts for delays, gate changes, take-off and landing.
  • Lets two people who have connected with each other see each other's upcoming flights.
  • Keeps a log of past flights with distance and route statistics.

Google account access

You can sign in to Contrail with a Google account. If you choose to, you can also connect Gmail and Google Calendar so Contrail can find your flight bookings. That access is read-only and is used only to find flight confirmations and flight events for your own account. The details are in the privacy policy.
