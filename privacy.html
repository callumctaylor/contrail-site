Contrail

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Contrail is a private flight tracking app used by its developer and a small number of invited people. This page explains what data it handles and why.

What Contrail collects

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google, Contrail receives your name and email address to identify your account.
  • Flights. Flight number, date, route, booking reference, seat and cabin for flights you add or import, plus live status for those flights from a flight data provider.
  • Device tokens. A notification token for your phone so alerts can be delivered.

Gmail and Google Calendar

Connecting Gmail or Google Calendar is optional and is granted separately for each address you choose. Access is read-only.

  • Contrail searches your mail for airline and travel booking confirmations and reads your calendar for flight events, in order to add those flights to your own account.
  • Only the flight details listed above are saved. Other email and calendar content is not stored.
  • Contrail never sends, deletes or changes email or calendar events.
  • A small number of booking emails that cannot be read automatically may be processed by an AI model solely to extract the flight details. That content is not used to train AI models.

Contrail's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide the features described here.

Sharing

If you connect with another Contrail user, they can see the status of your upcoming flights. They cannot see your booking references. You can remove a connection at any time. Data is not shared with anyone else.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a private database with access restricted to your own account. Google access tokens are stored encrypted on the server and never on your phone.

Removing your data

You can disconnect a Google account in Settings, which revokes Contrail's access at Google. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting a flight removes it from Contrail. To have your whole account removed, ask the person who invited you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
